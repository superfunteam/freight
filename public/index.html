How to play

  1. The train rolls by. Empty cars come in from the right. Fill every one before it leaves the yard.
  2. Drag cargo onto a car. Two crates wait above the track, two below. Drop one on any empty car (or tap a crate, then tap a car).
  3. Mind the rules. The dispatcher adds rules as you go, like Onions can't ride next to Wood. Break one and it bounces off.
  4. It only gets faster. Some cars roll in already loaded. Miss a car or break a rule and you lose a ❤️. Three strikes and the train's gone.
